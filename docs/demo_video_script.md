# Demo Video Script - 3 to 5 minutes

## 0:00-0:30 | The help-desk moment

**On screen:** A simple help-desk counter scene, then the real Sanket Setu screen.

**Voiceover:** "A visitor arrives at a care desk. They sign; the staff member does not know ISL. Sanket Setu is a communication-support prototype that gives both people another way to understand each other. It is not a replacement for an interpreter."

## 0:30-1:20 | Visitor to staff

**On screen:** Start the live local camera. Show a supported numeral pose. Point out the engine label and the Hindi/English result.

**Voiceover:** "The current camera baseline reads a small set of hand shapes and numerals. The visitor sees the reading and confirms it before it joins the session. We have no trained classifier yet, so we do not claim sign-language translation or model accuracy."

## 1:20-2:15 | Two-way counter communication

**On screen:** Select a visitor message-board card, confirm it, and show the large Hindi/English output. Then tap a staff quick reply. If the browser supports it, demonstrate the optional Hindi/English voice-to-text control.

**Voiceover:** "If the camera does not recognize a pose, the visitor can choose a clearly labelled message manually. These cards are not sign predictions. Staff can answer with quick replies, or start the microphone to show speech as large text. Browser speech recognition may use its own provider; the app does not save audio."

## 2:15-2:50 | Uncertain means uncertain

**On screen:** Show an unsupported pose or select Try again. Point to the status text. Show the separate manual message board.

**Voiceover:** "The prototype should refuse to label unsupported signs. A manual phrase is always a user choice, never an AI guess. For urgent or high-stakes discussions, use a qualified interpreter and confirm all details with the visitor."

## 2:50-3:30 | What exists today

**On screen:** Show the vocabulary and model status in Signer mode, then Export notes and Clear.

**Voiceover:** "Our model directory and processed dataset are empty today. No accuracy has been measured, and no sign has been reviewed by a Deaf signer or qualified ISL interpreter. We are showing a working camera baseline and a two-way communication interface, while keeping the limit visible. Conversation notes stay in this page until the operator exports or clears them."

## 3:30-4:00 | Next steps and close

**On screen:** Return to the live screen and tagline.

**Voiceover:** "Next, we need Deaf signer and interpreter review, consented examples from multiple signers, and held-out evaluation before we expand the camera vocabulary. Sanket Setu: Har sanket, ab samajhne ke aur kareeb. Thank you."

## Recording checklist

- Record the real application, not a mock-up.
- Do not imply that manual message cards were recognized from signs.
- Do not claim the current camera recognizes Doctor, Medicine, Pain, Help, or other untrained labels.
- Do not show invented metrics, user tests, or endorsements.
- Request camera or microphone access only when demonstrating that feature.
- Caption the recording and keep the limitation statement visible.
